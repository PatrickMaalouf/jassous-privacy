# Privacy Policy for Jassous

**Last updated: October 11, 2026**

Jassous ("the app," "we," "our") is a local, pass-and-play party game.
This policy explains what data the app handles. The game itself runs
entirely on your device; the only network activity comes from the ads
the app shows (see [Advertising](#advertising)).

## Summary

- Gameplay is fully offline. Player names, categories, words, votes,
  and results never leave your device, and no internet connection is
  needed to play.
- The app shows ads from Google AdMob: one full-screen ad after every
  third finished round. AdMob may collect device and usage data to
  serve and measure those ads (details below).
- We do not run our own servers, and we do not collect, store, or sell
  any personal information ourselves.
- There is no account creation or sign-in.
- There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs in the app.
- If you are in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom, or
  another region that requires it, the app asks for your consent before
  any ad is requested, and you can change your choice at any time.

## What the app stores on your device

Everything in this table stays in the app's own private storage on your
device. None of it is sent to us or to anyone else, and none of it
identifies you.

| Data | Purpose | Persisted? |
|---|---|---|
| Player names typed at Setup | Shown on player cards, the vote tally, and the reveal screen during the current session | No — kept only in memory while the app is open; cleared when the app is closed |
| Custom categories/words you add | Let you play with your own categories for the session | No — kept only in memory while the app is open |
| Selected app language | Remembers your language choice (Arabic/English/French) | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| Selected word language | Remembers your word-display language choice, independent of the app's language | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| Selected theme (Light/Dark/System) | Remembers your display preference | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| Whether you've seen the onboarding tutorial | Avoids showing it again after your first launch | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| Whether you've confirmed the adult-content warning | Avoids re-showing that warning after you've confirmed it once | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| Which built-in words have already been played (word IDs only, no names or other details) | Avoids repeating the same secret word across rounds until every word has had a turn | Yes — locally, on this device only |
| How many rounds you have finished (a single number) | Decides when the next ad is due (every third round) | Yes — locally, on this device only |

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its data, removes all of it.

## Advertising

Jassous shows ads using **Google AdMob**, a service provided by Google
LLC. After every third finished round, the app may show one full-screen
ad on the screen between rounds. Ads are never shown while a round is
in progress.

To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its partners may collect and
process information from your device, such as:

- your device's advertising identifier (for example, the Android
  Advertising ID);
- your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
- device and software details (model, operating system version,
  language, app version);
- how ads are shown and interacted with, and diagnostic information
  about the ads SDK.

This information is collected by Google, not by us, and is handled
under Google's own policies. We do not receive it and cannot identify
you from it. See
[How Google uses data from sites or apps that use its services](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)
and [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) for
details.

Ads require an internet connection. If you are offline, or an ad fails
to load, no ad is shown and the game continues normally.

### Consent and your ad choices

- **Regions that require consent.** In the EEA, the UK, and other
  regions where the law requires it, the app shows a consent form
  (provided by Google's User Messaging Platform) the first time it
  runs. No ads are requested until you have made a choice that allows
  them. In those regions the app's **Settings → Privacy & ad choices**
  entry lets you review or change that choice at any time.
- **Personalized ads.** Where you have not consented to personalized
  ads, you may still see non-personalized ads.
- **Device-level controls.** You can reset your advertising ID or opt
  out of ads personalization in your device's settings (on Android:
  Settings → Privacy → Ads).

## Other third parties

Besides Google AdMob, the app integrates no third-party SDK: no
analytics, no crash reporting, and no cloud services. If that ever
changes in a future version, this policy will be updated first, and the
change will be reflected in the app stores' data-safety disclosures
before it ships.

## Children's privacy

Jassous is a general-audience party game and is **not directed at
children**, and the app is configured so that ads are not treated as
child-directed. It includes an optional 18+ content category (explicit
slang) that requires an explicit confirmation before it can be
selected; hosts are responsible for deciding whether that content is
appropriate for their group. We do not knowingly collect personal
information from children. If you believe a child has provided
personal information through the app, please contact us at the address
below.

## Your choices and rights

We hold no personal information about you, so there is nothing for us
to export or delete. You can:

- clear the app's data or uninstall it to remove every local setting
  listed above;
- change or withdraw your ad consent in **Settings → Privacy & ad
  choices** (where shown), or reset your advertising ID in your device
  settings;
- exercise any data rights you have regarding AdMob's processing
  (access, deletion, objection) with Google, through the links above.
